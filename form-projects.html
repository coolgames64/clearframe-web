Project briefs

Courtyard House

Brief. Bring daylight into the middle of a deep family home.

Design response. Set an open courtyard between the kitchen and living room so both can borrow light from a new exterior edge.

The Workshop

Brief. Make room for a workbench without letting tools take over the living space.

Design response. Put the bench by the window for task light and give tools a full-height storage wall within reach.

Riverside Apartment

Brief. Keep the river view visible from the living and dining areas.

Design response. Group tall storage along the internal wall and keep furniture low beside the glazing.
