Stoneware care

Wash, dry and store.

Hand-wash with warm water and a soft cloth. Let pieces dry fully before stacking, and avoid sudden changes in temperature.

Common questions

How should I stack bowls?

Stack bowls of similar sizes and use a thin cloth between pieces if you want to protect the glaze.

What if the glaze looks slightly different?

Small changes in tone can appear across glazed pieces, especially where the glaze gathers around an edge.
