How a project takes shape

Listen

Walk through the space and talk about what works, what gets in the way and how you want it to feel.

What to bring to a first meeting

Start with what you have. Formal drawings can wait.

  • A few photos of the rooms you want to change.
  • One daily routine you’d like the space to make easier.
  • A rough budget range and any dates you’re working around, if you know them.

Explore

Compare layouts and understand how each option changes light, circulation and storage.

Detail

Resolve materials, fittings and the small decisions that make the rooms easier to use.
