Process

From first email to launch.

We’ll agree what you need, share a preview and check the site together before it goes live.

  1. Tell me what you need

    Send a few lines about your business, your customers and what the website should help them do.

  2. Agree the scope

    We work out the pages, features, what you’ll supply and a price before work starts.

  3. Review the design

    I build a preview you can look through and we work through the feedback agreed in your quote.

  4. Get ready to launch

    I check the key pages and links with you, then we agree when and where the site goes live.

Ready to talk websites?

A short email about your business and the kind of site you need is enough to get started.
