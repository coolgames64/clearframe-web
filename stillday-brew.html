The pour-over guide

Your first filter brew.

  1. Start with 20 g of medium-ground coffee. Rinse your paper filter and tip out the rinse water.
  2. Pour about 50 g of hot water. Wet all the grounds and give them 30 seconds to bloom.
  3. Pour to 320 g in total. Let it drain, swirl the carafe and taste.
